# 🍬 SweetPOS

## Billing & Inventory Management System

SweetPOS is a retail management application designed for businesses such as sweet shops and dairy stores.

The application focuses on billing, inventory, stock management, and day-to-day retail operations.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

- Billing
- Inventory management
- Stock tracking
- GST-related billing workflows
- Dashboard
- Mobile support
- Offline workflows
- Business reporting
